Itinerary
Day 1 Arrival + Reykjavik City Walk
Arrive in Iceland and explore the colorful streets of Reykjavik. Visit the iconic Hallgrímskirkja church and the Sun Voyager sculpture.
Day 2 Blue Lagoon Geothermal Spa
Relax in the milky-blue, mineral-rich waters of the world-famous Blue Lagoon, surrounded by black lava fields.
Day 3 Golden Circle Tour
Experience Iceland's most famous route: see the erupting Geysir, the thundering Gullfoss waterfall, and the historic Thingvellir National Park.
Day 4 Glacier Hike + Ice Caves
Strap on crampons and grab an ice axe for a guided hike across a massive glacier, culminating in a visit to a stunning blue ice cave.
Day 5 Northern Lights Chase
Head out into the dark, remote countryside with an expert guide to hunt for the mesmerizing, dancing colors of the Aurora Borealis.
Day 6 Snaefellsnes Peninsula
Explore the 'Iceland in Miniature' peninsula, featuring dramatic sea cliffs, black sand beaches, and the Kirkjufell mountain.
Day 7 Whale Watching Tour
Set sail from Reykjavik harbor on a specialized boat to spot minke whales, humpbacks, and playful dolphins in their natural habitat.
